# Lyric Loop

Loop a beat and write lyrics in one place. Works on iPhone, Android and desktop browsers.

**Open the app:** https://andocalrizzian.github.io/lyric-loop/

On iPhone, open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

- Loop 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars; jump by phrase or bar
- Slow down to 0.5× or 0.75× without changing pitch
- Lyrics split into sections (verse, hook, bridge…) that save automatically
- Save a .txt copy to Files or Google Drive

Lyrics and beats stay on your own device. Nothing is uploaded.
